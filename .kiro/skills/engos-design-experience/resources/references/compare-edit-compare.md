# Revise without losing the stronger whole

Use for a material change or uncertain design advantage. Keep a recoverable draft. A small correction needs a focused check, not a review ritual.

## Inspect both the task and the composition

Use the rendered artifact in the actual task state after the last edit, including relevant connected states. Correct functional blockers, then inspect the decision where it happens: selection or draft changed, relevant control focused, and commitment or recovery available. Is the baseline/current basis accessible? Does a claim carry its decisive qualification? Can the person continue after editing, opening detail, or recovering from failure? Check affected narrow and keyboard-focused states when relevant to the target.

Also inspect what the proposed improvement costs. Compare readable option coverage, repeated content, hierarchy, qualifier prominence, and the path to the action at the same viewport and state. More context can move the action farther away, narrow comparison columns, or reduce the alternatives available to scan. Scroll depth alone is not failure; displaced evidence, unreadable density, or needless travel may be.

Full-page images establish eventual content availability. They do not establish what fits in the actual viewport or how focus and return behave. Use the relevant evidence for each claim.

## Get another perspective when it can change the work

Existing user feedback may be enough. Where independent inspection would expose a meaningful blind spot, provide the task, artifact, captured working-state evidence, constraints, and access without a preferred verdict. Ask the reviewer to identify the concrete obstacle and the strengths worth preserving. No reviewer quota or automatic agent sequence is required.

Separate observed behavior, plausible consequence, preference, and unknowns. An appearance claim needs an inspected render; a behavior claim needs interaction evidence. An independent agent is not a human study. If independent feedback is unavailable, continue useful author inspection and name that limit.

## Edit the cause and compare with the draft

When a condition disappears during a decision, attach a concise qualification to the claim. When repeated descriptions crowd the comparison, keep the decisive exchange visible and preserve full evidence through useful detail access. When input vanishes, fix state ownership. Do not treat every criticism as a request to add another visible element.

Compare the revision with the recoverable draft on the affected task, states, and composition. Retain the better whole version, including reversions or a smaller correction. Renew only checks whose dependencies changed or whose validity new evidence challenges. Expand review when unresolved concerns justify it, not to repeat a ceremony.

Stop when the authorized outcome is supported, available time/access prevents further justified work, or another revision lacks a concrete expected benefit. Report material gaps; do not chase a score or continue sampling until a preferred judgment appears.

## Keep readiness precise

Check relevant input, focus and return, labels/status, contrast, scaling, and motion in the actual target. [WCAG](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and [ARIA APG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/) are web references, not native proof or evidence of full conformance.

A sketch may support direction selection. A connected mock can establish inspected simulated behavior. Live operation establishes only what was verified against the real service. “The condition is visible with the estimate” is an observation; “people decide faster” requires a suitable human study.
