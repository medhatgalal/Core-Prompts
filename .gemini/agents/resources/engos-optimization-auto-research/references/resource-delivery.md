# Resource Delivery for Auto-Research

Before a resource-dependent workflow, load its declared route completely through the bundled loader or actual file-read tools. Skill resources live under the skill directory's `resources/`; agent resource paths are relative to the directory containing bundled `capability.json`, without another `resources/` prefix.

Use `python3 "<resource-root>/scripts/load_module.py" --route experiment --format text` for experiment work, `templates` for scaffolding, `bootstrap` for the convenience helper, or `promotion` for promotion preparation. The loader supplies complete selected content and dependency identities. Its output is evidence of assembly/delivery when actually supplied to the invocation, not proof of comprehension.

Keep shared rules, selected workflow guidance, and required templates available before dependent work. Give independent reviewers the relevant complete contents. Missing, changed, stale, or truncated resources require recovery before proceeding; a filename, hash, self-authored receipt, or an earlier agent's assurance is not a substitute. Never fabricate a run or claim a resource was read without actual supplied content. Resource identity belongs in working evidence, not an invented user-facing verification claim.

Template materialization is scaffolding only. Reading or executing bootstrap.py does not run experiments or satisfy promotion. Scope, budget, evaluation integrity, and user/host authority continue to govern all routes.
