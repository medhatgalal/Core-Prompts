# Intake and frame fields

These are authoring fields, not duplicated G0/G1 predicates. Keep original,
candidate and accepted identities explicit. Unknown values remain pending with
question/uncertainty IDs. Never fill placeholders with invented answers.

## brief.md

- Run and source IDs, original request or approved precise reference, date/revision.
- Verbatim critical constraints, exclusions, authority and requested endpoint.
- Source classification and permitted storage/export; originals preserved in place.
- Supplied suggestions clearly unselected; later decisions linked, not rewritten
  into the original statement.

## intake.md

- Identity: run, work order, generation, original source IDs/hashes.
- Understood problem: affected people and desired change, with evidence/attribution.
- Known: sourced facts and exact inspected coverage.
- Assumed: attributed interpretations to verify, never silently promoted to facts.
- Missing: gaps, why each matters, stable uncertainty IDs and candidate questions.
- Input directions not yet selected: quoted mechanisms and their source.
- Sources: read/partial/unreadable, freshness and conflicting claims.
- Next step: mode proposal, one to three consequential questions, effort checkpoint.

## framed.md

- Identity: run/generation, current G0 receipt and intake/source bindings.
- Problem and affected people; guiding user/scenario; why-now with evidence or
  attributed intent; business win and observable success; desired outcome.
- Appetite: actual willingness to spend, unit/bound/scope, decision ID and authority;
  explicitly pending if unconfirmed. Keep estimates and session effort separate.
- Walk-away: unacceptable cost/outcome/condition, actual decision and provenance.
- Boundaries: In/Out/Later outcomes, hard constraints and sources, exclusions and
  their decision IDs, without selected architecture or implementation steps.
- Uncertainties: stable IDs, linked question versions, affected decision/dependency,
  blocking/advisory reason, evidence requirement, respondent/owner or unassigned,
  status and proposed next action.
- Human decisions: accepted references, candidate events, relay/direct distinction,
  dissent, pending authority; follow the conductor's immutable snapshot contract.
- Handoff: what is settled, what awaits humans, technical research questions,
  relevant source access and effort remaining; proposed G1 review, no self-pass.

Frame-only completion after actual G1 supplies the frame and open research needs.
It does not imply a selected solution, investment approval beyond the recorded
decision, feasibility proof, publication or a human bet.
