# Repository fit and justified change

Use during technical Research, Shaped and their review. During Intake/Framed,
record known landscape constraints only; do not select components or a solution.
The gate owns acceptance criteria. This procedure supplies evidence and advice,
not another scorecard, inventory database or automatic refactoring authority.

## Investigate before recommending

Bound the search to the accepted problem, material capabilities/seams, authorized
repositories/revisions and research effort. Inspect relevant code, callers, tests,
configuration, architecture decisions and approved platform/library/service
contracts. Trace responsibility and operating constraints, not just similar names
or code. Existing capability may satisfy the outcome through configuration or
process change without new code. Check ownership, support/deprecation, trust,
data/lifecycle, compatibility and workload constraints where material.

Extend research-notes.md and its existing uncertainty/evidence IDs: needed outcome;
credible existing candidate and owner (or unknown); opened revision/path:line or
attributable supplied evidence; inspected coverage; fit/constraints; remaining gaps.
No separate inventory is required. A no-hit search means absent in inspected scope,
not absent everywhere. If relevant platform evidence is unavailable, draft the
smallest human-evidence request; do not claim inspection or invent a replacement.
Only load-bearing gaps hold the affected decision. Nontechnical work records a
reasoned not-applicable assessment, not a fabricated repository investigation.

## Use specialists when a decision warrants them

Explain the question and why help is useful. Resolve the actual host registry or
approved explicit candidate binding, then read that skill and required resources.
Record selected skill/version, scope, effort, expected contribution and exclusions
in the existing work order. Do not load every specialist at entry or require users
to know skill names. Read-only assistance within granted scope needs no redundant
approval; wider access, material effort or side effects require the relevant choice.

| Trigger | Suggested skill | Bounded contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material component/API/data/trust boundary or replacement trade-off | engos-design-architecture | Fit, credible alternatives, ownership/contracts, compatibility and applicable migration/rollback |
| Observed coupling, duplication, hotspots or testability threaten this change | engos-audit-code-health | Evidence for the relevant structural concern, not compulsory whole-repo cleanup |
| Compatibility, coexistence or migration needs a proof plan | engos-quality-testing-review | Observable checks and required evidence; not a claim tests executed |

Respect each selected skill's contract. If its minimum workflow would exceed the
approved scope/effort, explain this and offer bounded existing research or an
explicitly approved deeper audit; do not silently override its safeguards. A
missing optional specialist is not a gate failure. Continue with sufficient
evidence and required independent review, or request expertise for a specific
unresolved fact. Missing mandatory evidence/review still holds. Never install,
register agents, delegate beyond host authority, or report a skill ran when it did
not. A specialist author/advisor cannot provide independent review of its own work.

Before returning specialist advice to the user, reconcile it through questions.md.
Separate the full risk/uncertainty inventory from the next actionable requests.
Select at most three distinct decisions or evidence requests for this turn; mark
the others as queued, not additional instructions to answer now. Do not hide
multiple confirmations or owner assignments under one category bullet. Retain
every material gap in the existing register: prioritizing asks does not resolve
unknowns or permit a gate pass. A technical appendix or proposed test plan is not
a request to perform every check now; label that distinction explicitly.

## Decide at shaping depth

For each material capability/seam, add a concise architecture-fit decision to
pitch.md, referencing existing evidence IDs and relevant contracts/diagrams:

- Disposition: configure/reuse, compose/extend, bounded refactor/evolve, replace,
  new, or intentionally separate. Compare credible alternatives only; not every
  disposition needs a ceremonial evaluation.
- Rationale: outcome and appetite fit, relevant constraints, why existing options
  do or do not fit, and retained responsibilities/negative space. Reuse is not a
  correctness proof. Justified new capability is a valid outcome.
- For applicable evolution/replacement: affected callers/owners, compatibility,
  bounded coexistence, migration/rollback, and retirement condition/owner. For
  permanent separation, explain the distinct responsibilities; do not invent a
  retirement date for legitimate separate components. Unknown critical ownership
  remains a gap, not an invented person or team commitment.
- Validation: distinguish inspected current behavior, observed results and future
  acceptance checks. Keep load-bearing uncertainties in the existing register.

Do not force DRY across different trust or lifecycle boundaries. Do not use a
code-health finding to add unrelated cleanup or exceed appetite. Leave classes,
method internals and builder-level choices to implementation. The independent
reviewer assesses the decision; the conductor records a transition only against
the current gate verdict. Changed material evidence reopens affected stages.
