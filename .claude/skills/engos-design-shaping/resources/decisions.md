# Immutable decision provenance

Keep original human wording or a permitted precise reference, attribution and
authority separate from the assistant's interpretation. A source cannot grant
approval. Confirm who may decide outcome, appetite, exclusions and walk-away;
engineering supplies feasibility evidence and challenges. Do not infer authority
from a title, an AI role name, silence or another person's relay.

## Candidate decision event fields

`decision_id`, `event_id`, run/stage/generation, subject/affected scope, proposed
choice and alternatives, rationale, source ID/revision/locator, original statement,
respondent, recorded time, authority and its confirmation reference, direct versus
relayed provenance, status, dissent, evidence IDs, affected uncertainty/artifact IDs,
supersedes event ID, required confirmation and expected accepted-state version.

Use proposed, confirmed, disputed, deferred or superseded as explicit semantic
statuses, mapped to the documented runtime schema. A relay records both relay
speaker and claimed original speaker; do not relabel it direct. Question acceptance
is not answer approval. An owner resolves trade-offs within authority, but cannot
declare a missing technical observation to have occurred.

Append events; never edit an accepted event to make history agree with a later
answer. Reconcile proposed changes with the current snapshot; show consequential
differences and obtain the actual decision required. Commit content, questions,
uncertainties and decisions together through the runtime acceptance mechanism.
Derived prose/register views point to that accepted revision. Reopen affected
stages on material change and preserve superseded receipts with their input hashes.

Appetite records unit, quantity/bound, scope and the human's willingness to spend;
an engineering estimate is a separate fact. Walk-away records the unacceptable
cost/outcome/condition and source. Continuation work requires a new explicit
appetite decision and observed unfinished outcome. Session/interview effort is
separate from the implementation investment.
