# Framed dispatch

Use the conductor's common work-order envelope and prompt; append:

> Act as the framer after current G0 acceptance. Read intake, original constraints,
> source coverage and candidate/accepted human decisions with their provenance.
> Draft framed.md using templates.md. Establish who is affected, problem, why-now,
> desired outcome and observable success; use actual confirmed appetite, walk-away
> and scope authority. Keep missing decisions pending. Ask only consequential
> unanswered questions using the assistance ladder and effort checkpoint. Propose
> engineering challenges as constraints/failure questions, without selecting a
> mechanism. Return a candidate frame, question/uncertainty and decision events,
> exact blockers and G1 review request. Do not accept state, solve the architecture
> or treat Framed as feasibility proof or a staffing commitment.

Inspect every paragraph/table for hidden how-to choices. “Survives reload” is an
outcome; naming a persistence engine selects a solution. “No changes to the other
team's service” is a boundary; “use its polling endpoint” chooses a mechanism.
Keep relevant sourced existing-system constraints with evidence and explain their
effect; move unselected source suggestions back to intake, preserving history.

Read accessible evidence before asking facts again. Product and engineering may
accept, edit or reject questions with reasons. Rejection does not close the stable
uncertainty. Actual appetite and walk-away need human decisions; unresolved
technical feasibility questions may pass to Research under the gate's criteria.
Finish independent framing work while awaiting replies, then return a resumable
hold; never select answers merely to meet a question limit.
