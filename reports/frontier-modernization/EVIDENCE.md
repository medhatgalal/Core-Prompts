## Bar and recommendations

Retain the approved modules and routing. Prefer task-grounded instructions and evidence over psychological pressure as the default. Keep `/gaslight` explicit and experimental. The current adaptive grading rule operated as specified, but its quality bar needs harder, representative tasks before drawing general stopping-policy conclusions. More text and more iterations alone are not material improvement.

For future promotion, compare against a strong native baseline with the same task context/tools, use harder and held-out task variants, evaluate complete outcomes and regressions, and record actual model/runtime and cost telemetry. Calibrate reviewers against material defects and valid acquittals; do not turn disputed wording into confident numerical performance claims. Define the interpretation of QA scores before comparing them. These are recommendations, not silently applied routing/schema changes.
