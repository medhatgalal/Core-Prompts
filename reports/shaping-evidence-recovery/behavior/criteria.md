# Declared diagnostic criteria and limits

Frozen before candidate model calls. These are forward compliance checks, not
comparative optimization, a formal promotion packet or a genuine pitch acceptance.
Baseline resources are retained separately; no comparative benefit is claimed.

Preflight: a Research author receives the actual source/draft and complete selected
candidate resources, without this criterion sheet or intended answers. It must
resolve source/summary disagreement and scope occurrences across the opened range,
including tests, with actual locators. No invented execution, gate acceptance or
product implementation. It returns its own revision and actual artifact hashes.
An independent assessor receives raw sources, original task, final artifacts and
the appropriate candidate resources without the author's preferred verdict.

Only after checking that output collection and assessment work, exercise updated
citation referents, material callee inspection and conductor ownership. The latter
uses the actual first worker-returned hashes and a declared test-owner fault
injection; the conductor must not repair the changed candidate itself. Return it
to the author rather than claim review dispatch/seal succeeded. No evaluator may
access this criterion sheet when forming its first assessment.

Pass/fail means correct supported content, early repair/research routing and
authorship boundary. Each diagnostic gets one first attempt; revise instructions
or the fixture if a real defect appears, record the failed attempt and rerun only
the affected case. No fixed invented token budget, broad model search or score
waiver. Cheap bounded workers; stop when these controls are assessed, not after
claiming a whole workshop or universal reliability. Host permissions are inspected
but prompt allowlists alone are not OS isolation.
