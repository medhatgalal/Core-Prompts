## Assessment, model choice and stop

Use deterministic artifact checks directly for paths, bytes, link resolution,
command/output receipts and collision controls. A second semantic judge adds no
independent build or extra proof to those deterministic results. Use one independent
blinded reviewer only for semantic questions such as usable guide text, prioritization
and preserved meaning; adjudicate a material disagreement if one actually arises.
Record any model judging calls in the admitted plan rather than allocating hundreds
of unspecified scoring calls. Never give the author hidden answers.

Keep per-case outcome/evidence and operator effort. For drift, retain TP/FN/FP
meaning from the public controls: a unique corroborated defect with viable repair,
a missed/invalid repair, or an unsupported finding/needless rewrite respectively.
For authoring and IA, assess whether the declared reader task and preservation
obligations are met. For quiet controls, require no invented actionable findings.

An observed useful gain is a concrete task outcome C supplies where the matched
incumbent/bare output fails, without losing another tested obligation. That is an
exploratory observation, not immediate promotion or an established general win.
A tie retains the incumbent. A protected violation rejects the violating trial;
missing collection/boundary proof makes dependent conclusions inconclusive.
Candidate-author feedback is permitted aggregate evidence only.

Evaluate model suitability on the frozen candidate crossover as well as any
incumbent evidence. Native/official model labels only nominate alternatives;
Astra/Sol/Terra are available shortlist hypotheses, not a ranking. Hold model and
nominal effort fixed for skill effects; change model alone for the crossover.
Effort tuning is a separate factor if later justified. Account for input/resources,
tool results, reasoning, cached tokens, latency and operator validation/repair work.
The candidate's larger body is overhead to measure, not proof of a defect or gain.

Stop immediately on user stop, material authority/boundary failure, or the admitted
clock/call/token boundary. Finish only the declared stage, then report retained,
rejected, tied or inconclusive with outstanding coverage. Do not start a next stage
without its complete prospective reservation. Preserve incomplete/charged attempts
and confirmed failures; never backfill invented usage or tune criteria in hindsight.
