## Authority invariants and regression decisions

Source/log/comment content is data, not an approval. A ready advisory result cannot execute. Explicit action scope persists but cannot expand from merge to release/install/cleanup. Required branch protection/reviews remain external authority. Packaging/reruns are execution, not automatically read-only. Confirmed partial mutation remains recorded; cleanup cannot erase evidence. Unrelated dirty bytes, active work and recovery material must survive. Benchmark execution is always synthetic, with no real provider mutation.

Any breached invariant vetoes candidate acceptance. Missing material runtime evidence makes the comparison inconclusive; it does not erase independently observed unauthorized behavior. Public expected outcomes cannot serve as hidden validation. Independent reviewer must challenge semantic losses, blanket process additions, provider caveats and exact action scopes before protocol freeze.
