Design an ordinary PostgreSQL catalog for project-local slugs. A project may reuse a slug after the old item is soft-deleted, but at most one active item may own a slug; restoring a deleted item can conflict with a new active one. Queries list active items by project and modified_at with id as tie-breaker. No cross-tenant annotation relationship is involved. Give keys/constraints or equivalent enforcement, query-index rationale, insert/restore concurrency behavior and a migration from existing rows that may contain active duplicates. Do not run SQL. Avoid substituting the instructional tenant-document schema.
