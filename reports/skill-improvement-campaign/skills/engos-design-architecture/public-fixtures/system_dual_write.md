Design ingestion for files, SQL metadata and a search index. Object storage, SQL and the queue have separate commits; no distributed transaction is available. Workers receive events at least once and may crash after updating the index but before acknowledgment. Tenants can request deletion while old jobs remain queued or are later replayed. Search may lag by 30 seconds; accepted uploads must not silently disappear. The team has two engineers on call. Specify durable states/commit and acknowledgment order, duplicate/replay/deletion policy, recovery ownership, observability and rollout. Compare a minimal viable design with a more elaborate alternative. Do not claim exactly-once end-to-end behavior without a mechanism.
