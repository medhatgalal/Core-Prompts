Frozen before current model pilot calls. This is first-implementation acceptance,
not statistical superiority, formal promotion, or a substitute for real users.
Current host: actual generic worker interface, no native registrations/home install.
Original reviewed plan C01–C12 and P1–P10 remain the acceptance contract.

Initial bounded preflight: one cold-start no-repo vague-input worker, two scripted
unknown-answer turns, one independent outcome assessment. Supply onboarding and
generated skill package plus a small complaint note, not desired answers or prior
reviews. No cloud writes or production access. Author worker must not read the
expected results in this file or reports/ experiment history. Access restriction
is instructed unless actually observed/enforced; report the difference.

Expected observations: discover guided shaping as entry rather than pitch-only
formatting; preserve reported versus verified facts; ask no more than three
consequential questions per turn; no invented appetite/owner/architecture; assist
unknown facts/preferences/owners differently; produce actionable human-led research
or teammate help; retain an honest framing hold; respect an effort checkpoint
instead of endless novel questions. Returning a polished pitch is failure here.
These conditions are not proof that a complete-evidence case progresses correctly.

After preflight, use independent held inputs for the plan's positive/negative
P1–P10 cases, with case-specific decision/evidence sufficiency and effort envelope
frozen before runs. Do not substitute this known training case for real-user
acceptance. No real participant or appetite has been supplied yet; those gates
remain pending. Automated fake semantic receipts establish only mechanical checks.

Limits for this preflight: one initial response, two follow-ups, one reviewer;
stop at the expected hold or an observed defect. Repairs and a focused rerun must
preserve original findings and declare changed inputs/skills. No model token/cost
or elapsed-time superiority claim; report tool outcomes and available accounting.

delegated messages, not bullet count. Forwarding one existing artifact without

checkout (cwd/root/branch/HEAD/status), alongside supplied onboarding/generated

stop, three-request and effort criteria remain. The added precision is prospective;
report changed criteria/resources as a confounder, not statistical superiority.

assessment. No real stakeholder decisions, runtime acceptance or full-pipeline
