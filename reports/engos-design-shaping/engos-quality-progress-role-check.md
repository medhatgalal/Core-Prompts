# engos-quality-progress-role-check

Preregistered focused simulation, not a comparative optimization or human-usability
study. The user assigned the controller as judge and separate junior/mid/senior
product/engineering subagents. Six fresh participants receive only one frozen
visible status-card screenshot each, role/task instructions and inherited host
rules. No raw JSON, source code, expected answer, previous grade or other role's
answer is supplied. They may inspect the assigned image; no external action.

Cases are explicit mechanical fixtures generated with FAKE semantic receipts in
the real journal and labelled Simulation. They are not a real G0–G4 pilot. Check
whether the visible card communicates its own state without interpreting JSON:
current stage, what is accepted, what blocks progress, assigned/needed ownership,
next permitted action, requested stopping point, and limits of snapshot/simulation.

- A: research held on missing caller-lifecycle evidence; no assigned respondent.
- B: requested framing-only finish reached; later gates outside scope.
- C: changed saved target; unchanged content remains accepted, overall finish pending.

Judge PASS only when no false advancement, invented owner, invalid restart or
real/live claim occurs, and the relevant next action is identified. Record any
confusing wording verbatim. Failure prompts a scoped repair, not a redefined bar.
Paired seniority/case assignment is not controlled comparison of experience levels.

Bound: six one-turn participant calls, no nested workers, no extra trial or mutation
in this batch. Actual clock anchor 2026-09-18 11:44:50 UTC; hard stop 12:04:50 UTC,
including dispatch/wait/judgment. At deadline stop new calls and
report incomplete work; local evidence closeout is separate. Cost/token usage is
unknown unless exposed by the host. Fresh contexts do not establish OS isolation.
