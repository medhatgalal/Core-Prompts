# engos-design-shaping worked examples

Purpose: teaching references of different complexity and vagueness, with actual
author/reviewer iterations. These four inputs are constructed teaching cases
informed by document comparison; they are not verbatim company requests or
proof of any company's implementation. Fixture facts define each hypothetical
system. Never describe fixture premises as observed production or spike results.

The earlier Continuous Update real-input attempt remains separate and pending.
The source-document comparison is private; this library contains generic scenarios.

## Matrix and fixed criteria

| Case | Complexity | Initial vagueness | Learning objective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| engos-example-model-capacity | Low | Low | Small API change without overengineering; distinguish absent data from zero |
| engos-example-insight-visibility | Low | High | Turn vague aspiration into one outcome; avoid inventing ranking/AI work |
| engos-example-config-deployment | High | Low | Preserve target overrides and old-package behavior across boundaries |
| engos-example-unified-chat | High | High | Narrow a broad experience request; retain an evidence-based stop for a critical seam |

Two rounds per case: initial author run, independent review, then case-specific
clarification/challenge and revision, followed by independent re-review. Preserve
both rounds and actual findings. The second round is not evidence of improvement
unless its assessed dimension improves; a correct repeated stop may be the result.

Use candidate-map.json and the full five-stage design. Author may self-check
G0-G2 with explicit observations; G3 requires independent review and render
inspection. Stop at failed gates. No author may invent a reviewer, user answer,
spike execution or surface receipt. Required decisions in the exercise source
are explicitly hypothetical scenario inputs, not new live investment approvals.

The compared material adds: guiding use case and business win, In/Out/Later,
ordered cut list, objective kill criterion, and clearance evidence for risks.
Each advanced shaped case must include these plus component/sequence/data-flow,
contracts, security owners, negative responsibilities, slices and source refs.

Results classify separately: document quality within the fixture; actual model
gate behavior; actual local rendering; actual cloud placement; live feasibility.
Only evidence in the corresponding class can satisfy it. A reference-ready
example is not a live production bet-ready verdict.

Budget: four cases, two author rounds each, two batch independent reviews and
one focused repair/recheck if required for critical false advancement. No
statistical superiority or provider-general reliability claim. No deployment,
source promotion, home installation or production implementation.
