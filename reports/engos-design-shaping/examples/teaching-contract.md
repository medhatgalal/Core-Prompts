# Teaching execution contract

This exercise mode applies only to the four explicitly constructed cases here.
It does not modify the live G1 decision requirement or the pending real-input run.

The controller's supplied scenario decisions are accepted hypothetical inputs
for teaching execution. Cite them as fixture decisions, never actual human/team
commitments. File/line inspection proves what the fixture states, not that a
production system behaves that way. No runtime spike may be invented.

Authors apply G0-G2 sequentially and record their own inspected conditions and
verdicts. A failed gate stops later-stage production. G3 includes independent
review plus actual local rendering/pixel inspection by the controller; author
outputs stop at review/render pending. G4 needs actual target evidence and cannot
be supplied by a fictional premise. Educational content approved against fixture
facts is labeled reference-ready, never certified as a live production bet.

For each round write a concise journal with evidence references, gate outcomes,
unresolved decisions and actual checks. Keep round one immutable when revising
round two. The examples are expected to include useful correct stops; not every
problem must reach a passing later gate. Compare the same rubric across rounds.

Add to shaped examples: guiding use case and business win; In/Out/Later; ordered
cut list with quality and security protected; objective kill criteria; and
clearance evidence for risks. These complement the original exemplar requirements.
