# Unified chat — Framed

Teaching frame; original statement and source identity retained in intake.md (`input.md:6`). G0 passed by author observation before this artifact.

**Problem and people.** People planning and building in the product encounter separate conversations and lose navigation context on reload (`input.md:14-15`).

**Why now.** U1 seeks continuity between planning and building; the fixture gives current friction but no deadline, usage numbers or measured business loss (`input.md:8-15`).

**Desired outcome / business win.** One persistent development conversation survives reload and connects planning with building. Less repeated orientation is the intended benefit, not measured savings (`input.md:8-9`, `input.md:14-15`).

**Appetite.** U1 supplies one month with two engineers as a hypothetical willingness to spend. Walk away or reshape if the usable core cannot fit that month (`input.md:8-10`).

**In.** Continuity across planning, building and reload. Seeing results remains part of the raw aspiration; preview feasibility and the exact result experience remain questions, not selected implementation (`input.md:6-9`, `input.md:21-22`, `input.md:26`).

**Out.** Cross-app work, an application-level planning product, broad performance hardening (`input.md:10-11`). Nothing further is silently excluded.

**Constraints.** ProductOwner owns outcome decisions; Engineering owns feasibility research (`input.md:16`). Existing session authentication is context, not proof of compatibility across modes (`input.md:18-22`). No fabricated API or prototype results (`input.md:28-30`).
