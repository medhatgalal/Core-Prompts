# Insight visibility scenario

Constructed teaching fixture. Facts are scenario premises, not company evidence.

Raw input: "Make insights more useful. We can't tell whether anyone cares."

Known context:
- Product managers currently request monthly aggregate adoption counts from support.
- Existing InsightService records aggregate viewed and generated counts per account
  per day; it also holds customer content that must not leave the product boundary.
- Internal MetricsPlatform accepts only approved aggregate-count schemas. It has
  an existing daily batch intake and product-manager access role.
- Publishing new customer content or creating new metrics is outside this example.
- There is no evidence in the fixture that ranking or insight quality is defective.

Missing: which outcome the requester wants, appetite, success measure and kill
criterion. Do not infer those decisions. Round one should expose the missing
framing decisions. The controller will provide a separate scenario clarification
for round two; it is not available during the first author run.
