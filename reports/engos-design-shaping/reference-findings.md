# engos-design-shaping reference findings

The source search located a separate Framed/Shaped pair, a group Shape Up pitch
template, shaping-alignment notes and three selected real pitches. This was a
bounded sample, not an exhaustive corpus review. The private source catalog and
revision-bound extracts remain in task-local storage.

Findings adopted in the teaching examples:

- Framing needs a guiding user/scenario, business win and success criteria.
- A supplied desired outcome can be concrete without selecting implementation.
  Distinguish it from a new mechanism introduced by the assistant.
- Keep In, Out and Later separate. No-Gos name tempting traps, not merely features
  excluded from a release.
- Include an ordered cut list and objective kill criteria; cuts do not waive
  quality, usability, security or the core outcome.
- Risks need clearance evidence. A missing linked spike cannot become a claim
  that the spike passed. A shaped title is not a gate result.
- Required investigation inside a document labeled ready is a contradiction to
  resolve. Retained superseded explanations also require reconciliation.
- Small apparent wiring changes can hide approval or history/backfill obligations.
  Complexity and vagueness must be varied independently in evaluation cases.

Application: examples/ contains four constructed cases and two author/reviewer
rounds per case. Their scenario premises are explicitly fictional. They are
teaching references informed by the comparisons, not assertions about a live
company implementation or replacements for the unfinished real-input exercise.
