Visually inspect the [original comparison](comparison-before.png) and [regrouped revision](comparison-after.png) when this question is relevant and tools permit. The inherited filenames “before” and “after” identify chronology, not winner and loser.

They do not establish autonomous skill uptake, model superiority, native behavior, or human performance. Development model reviewers found a substantial task-continuity advantage and a small visual/readability regression in the revision. Treat both findings as bounded judgments, not human validation or a reason to copy either layout.

## The task

Choose exactly two exhibitions within $60,000 and 280 staff hours, compare the draft with a saved plan, then save deliberately. Feasibility totals do not determine which qualitative exchange best serves the planner. Descriptions, operational notes, source facts, and saved/draft semantics must remain intact.

## What the original preserves

The original image gives the saved and draft plans room, aligns exact measures, and uses readable editorial hierarchy. The qualitative exchange follows below. In the full specimen, editing and saving were located elsewhere. This separation risks asking the planner to carry the exchange across locations; the image alone does not demonstrate a human failure.

## What the revision gains and spends

The revision brings editing, saved/draft context, limits, validity, comparison, and save into one working area. Conditions such as compatible rails accompany the relevant estimates. These relationships support reviewing an exchange while changing or committing it.

The cost is visible too: a utility summary competes with the reading area, comparison columns narrow, and full descriptions/notes repeat alongside catalogue material. The revision can support continuity while weakening density and readability. More simultaneous context is not automatically a better whole experience.

## The next decision is unresolved

A possible next treatment would keep compact saved/draft identity, exact changed measures, decisive qualitative differences, and short conditions visible; give full descriptions and notes a clear accessible home; and use recovered space for readable comparison and alternatives. That is a hypothesis, **not a third artifact or a proven improvement**.

It could fail if shortening the exchange drops an important condition, if unfamiliar choices need the full descriptions together, or if detail navigation loses the draft or comparison basis. Another platform may need a dedicated review step. Test the actual task and render before selecting a treatment.

The transferable lesson is to retain usable meaning while inspecting the cost of its presentation. Preserve source detail and real state semantics without assuming every paragraph must be repeated at every action. These screenshots establish only inspected browser appearance; save, recovery, keyboard operation, and human understanding need their own evidence.
